Layla's Cheat Sheet
Area & Perimeter — keep this next to you!
Fence vs Carpet
Perimeter = walk AROUND, add every outside side → ft
Area = cover INSIDE → ft² (little ²)
Parallelogram: A = b × h
"Slide the slice" — cut the triangle off, slide it over → a rectangle.
Triangle: A = ½ × b × h
"A triangle is half a parallelogram." Or b × h ÷ 2.
Trapezoid: A = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × h
"Add the parallels, times height, cut in half." (2 copies = 1 parallelogram)
Height stands up straight ⊾
Use the dashed height with the square corner. Slanted side → perimeter only. Height can be outside an obtuse triangle.
Weird shape? Split ➕ or cut ➖
Split into rectangles/triangles and add, OR big rectangle − the missing piece. Find missing sides: opposite sides match.
⚠️ Watch out: slanted side ≠ height · don't forget the ½ · ft vs ft² · height is never part of the perimeter · count every side on L-shapes (none twice)
Backwards: A = b × h → h = A ÷ b.   Triangle: h = A × 2 ÷ b.   Coordinates: length = big − small.   Always: formula → numbers → answer + unit ✏️
